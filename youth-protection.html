Youth Protection

The safety of the young men in DeMolay comes before everything else the program does. Here is how that is enforced in Missouri.

Missouri DeMolay operates under the DeMolay International Youth Protection Policy, received by DI's Board of Directors on 9 March 2024 and reviewed on 20 June 2024. It binds every jurisdiction, chapter, and adult volunteer. This page summarizes what it requires.

If a young man is in immediate danger, or a crime is in progress, call 911 first. Reporting to DeMolay comes after that, not before it. See how to report a concern.

The rules that apply at every meeting and event

  • Two-deep leadership. At least two registered advisors are present at every meeting, trip, outing, and chapter activity — without exception.
  • The rule of four. No activity runs with fewer than four people present. If someone is hurt, one adult can stay with him while two others go for help.
  • Visual access. Advisors do not isolate themselves with an individual member. A conversation that needs to be private is held where it cannot be overheard but can still be seen — an open room, an outdoor clearing. Closed doors, vehicles, and secluded spaces are off limits.
  • Electronic communication. When an advisor messages a member, a parent or a second advisor is included.
  • Physical contact. Advisors avoid unnecessary physical contact, including tickling and wrestling.
  • You are always told, and always welcome. Parents and guardians must be notified of every official activity, with the date, location, start and end times, and contact information for the advisors who will be there. Parents may attend anything their son attends.

How many adults have to be there

Two advisors is the floor for any activity, whatever the group size. Above that, the ratio depends on age:

So 10 DeMolays need two advisors; 12 need three; 22 need four. Ten Squires need three. The nature of the event can require more than the minimum.

Situations with extra rules

  • Private homes. An activity at a private home requires prior approval from the chapter's Advisory Council, written permission from the Executive Officer, and permission from every parent or guardian. Two-deep leadership still applies.
  • Overnight events. Require a written permission slip and a medical release form from each member's parent or guardian.
  • Transportation. Members arrange their own transport where possible. When an advisor drives, the first member picked up calls his parents and stays on the line until the next is picked up; the last member remaining calls his parents and stays on the line until he is home.
  • Photography. Photos and video must reflect the values of the organization, and permission is required before a member's likeness is published on official channels. DeMolay is not responsible for private photos attendees post to their own social media.
  • Social media. The Executive Officer has administrative access to every DeMolay-related account in the jurisdiction and can remove anyone who violates these policies.
  • Discipline. Constructive only. Corporal or physical punishment is forbidden.

Overnight housing

  • No adult shares a room with a member unless everyone in the room is immediate family. A legal guardian counts as a parent.
  • Members are roomed with members of similar age — preferably within two years.
  • Squires are not roomed with DeMolays unless they are related.
  • Where an adult and a member are in adjoining hotel rooms, the connecting door stays closed and locked on both sides unless they are related. In a multi-bedroom suite, a member may not share the suite with an unrelated adult.
  • A member does not stay overnight at an advisor's home unless they are directly related or the Executive Officer has given written permission.
  • Advisors supervise sleeping areas throughout the night, walking hallways and communal areas on a deliberately unpredictable schedule.

Every adult is screened before he or she serves

An adult cannot volunteer in any DeMolay program or activity until the whole process below is finished and DeMolay International has confirmed acceptance.

  1. Application. A written application and registration form, paperwork for a criminal background check and a motor vehicle history check, and three references not related by blood or marriage. Submitted through DI's e-scribe system.
  2. Review. The Executive Officer reviews the application and may contact references. He does not have to give a reason for rejecting one.
  3. Training. The applicant completes DI's advisor training program, plus any continuing education required.
  4. Background check. Run by a third-party vendor and reviewed with the Executive Officer, who may reject the applicant or restrict their service.

Anyone with a history of sexual crimes, child abuse, sexual molestation, or any crime involving children is permanently disqualified — as is anyone with pending charges involving children, a history of violence or sexually exploitative behaviour, or a termination from a paid or volunteer role for misconduct with a child or physical violence.

Registration is renewed annually and is not automatic — it lapses on 30 November each year and the Executive Officer can decline any renewal. Every advisor is background-checked at least once every five years, with additional random checks each year. Advisors must self-disclose any arrest, ticket, or adverse action taken against them by another youth organization, school, church, or day care within three business days.

What is prohibited outright

DeMolay has a zero-tolerance policy on all of the following, for members and adults alike. Violations can mean suspension or expulsion, and anything that breaks the law is referred to the authorities.

  • Physical violence — slapping, spanking, corporal punishment, or physical activity that creates unnecessary risk of injury.
  • Sexual contact or behaviour of any kind, including touching or exposing any area normally covered by a swimsuit. Advisors change and shower separately from members.
  • Sexually explicit material at any DeMolay activity.
  • Advisors dating or pursuing a romantic or sexual relationship with a member or any child.
  • Neglect — withholding food, water, or medical assistance as punishment.
  • Hazing and bullying, at events or anywhere else, including on DeMolay-related social media.
  • Alcohol, tobacco, vaping and e-cigarettes, illegal drugs, and misuse of prescription or over-the-counter medication.
  • Blindfolds and hoodwinks are prohibited in every DeMolay ceremony.
  • Secret activities, unofficial organizations, and any ceremony not published in the official rituals. Not even an Executive Officer can authorize one.

The policy also lists specific activities no chapter may run — among them tackle football, baseball, bungee jumping, skydiving, cliff diving, tree and free climbing, parkour, rodeo events, and a long list of shooting and projectile activities. Energy drinks are strongly discouraged.

How to report a concern

DeMolay defines abuse as any action, inaction, or event that endangers or injures the physical, psychological, or emotional well-being of any person.

Any member of DeMolay who has reason to believe a young man is being abused, bullied, or placed in danger must immediately inform the Executive Officer and follow Missouri's reporting requirements. Advisors receive training on those civil obligations in the first quarter of every year. DeMolay's internal process runs alongside the legal one — it does not replace it.

  1. If a young man is in immediate danger or needs medical attention, call 911. Send someone to meet the ambulance.
  2. Notify the lead advisor at the event — at a chapter meeting, the chapter chairman, or the chapter advisor if he is not present.
  3. Call the parents or guardians.
  4. Call the Executive Officer or their designee, and follow their direction.
  5. Once the young man is safe and being cared for, complete DeMolay International's incident report form — within 24 hours, and no later.

Write reports factually, avoid speculation, and use names rather than pronouns so it is clear who is being described. Do not run your own investigation, and do not discuss the incident with anyone else. Refer all other questions to the Executive Officer.

To raise a concern about conduct in a Missouri DeMolay chapter or event, contact the jurisdiction at info@modemolay.org (a monitored phone number will be added here). Suspected abuse should also be reported directly to local law enforcement or to the Missouri Child Abuse and Neglect Hotline, 1-800-392-3738.
