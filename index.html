For Parents & Guardians

The Questions
You Should Ask

Straight answers about safety, cost, time, and what DeMolay's relationship to Freemasonry actually means.

Is my son safe here?

Every adult who works with members is screened and trained under DeMolay International's youth protection program before they set foot in a chapter room. Our jurisdiction's full policy, reporting process, and the name of our youth protection officer are published here.

What does it cost?

Membership involves initiation and annual dues set by DeMolay International and by each chapter, plus optional fees for trips and events. Current amounts are listed in full, and no young man is turned away for inability to pay — ask any advisor about assistance.

How much time does it take?

Most chapters meet twice a month, with optional weekend events, service projects, and statewide gatherings through the year. Members choose how deep to go — and it's designed to fit around school, sports, and jobs.

Do we have to be Masons?

No. DeMolay is sponsored by Freemasonry and shares its values, but membership is open to any young man ages 9–21 regardless of his family's background or faith tradition. Members are asked to believe in a Supreme Being; beyond that, no particular religion is required or taught.
