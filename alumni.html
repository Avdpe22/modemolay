Once a DeMolay, Always a Senior DeMolay

If you were ever a member, you are a Senior DeMolay for life. Here is how to reconnect with the Order in Missouri.

Thousands of Missouri men came up through DeMolay. Many say the ritual, the friendships, and the first taste of real responsibility shaped what came after. The Order would like to hear from you again.

Ways to stay involved

  • Mentor. Spend time with a chapter a few times a year, sharing what your DeMolay years led to.
  • Sponsor a member. Cover dues or event costs for a young man who otherwise could not take part.
  • Join an advisory council. Put your experience to work in a chapter near you — see Volunteer.
  • Support the Order. Give to the causes DeMolay backs — see Donate.
  • Reconnect socially. Alumni gatherings, installations you can attend, and the annual State Convention.

The Legion of Honor

Senior DeMolays and adult leaders who have shown leadership in their careers, communities, and the Order may be recognized with the Legion of Honor, the Order's highest adult honor.
